# IoT Hunter — Community and Enterprise Licensing Policy

**Status: Draft for discussion. Not a binding offer or contract.**

## Community use proposal

The proposed future BUSL-1.1 Additional Use Grant would permit:

- Personal, independent use by individuals.
- Internal production use by organizations with up to 25 employees worldwide.
- Non-production evaluation, development, and testing as provided by the standard BUSL-1.1 terms.

The final license must define the employee-counting method, treatment of affiliates under common control, eligibility checks, and what happens if an organization grows beyond the threshold.

## Enterprise licensing proposal

Organizations above the 25-employee threshold would be asked to obtain a separate commercial license before production use.

Potential commercial agreement topics (not included automatically in BUSL-1.1):

- Named legal entity and covered affiliates.
- Permitted production environments and deployment scope.
- Support response targets and maintenance terms.
- Security updates and vulnerability disclosure process.
- Procurement, invoicing, renewal, and termination.
- Any separately negotiated redistribution, OEM, or hosted-service rights.

No price, service level, warranty, or support commitment is established by this draft.

## Contact

Commercial licensing contact details have not yet been designated. Do not publish a personal email address or promise commercial terms until the licensor has established an appropriate business contact and reviewed the agreement.
